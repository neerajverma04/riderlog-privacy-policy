RiderLog: Mileage Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to RiderLog: Mileage Tracker, developed by Neeraj Verma ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Android application.

RiderLog is designed for gig workers, delivery drivers, rideshare drivers, and self-employed individuals who need to track business mileage for tax deductions (IRS, CRA, HMRC, etc.).

Core Principle: RiderLog is built with a privacy-first approach. All core tracking and receipt scanning features work 100% on-device. Your trip data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to sync to cloud storage.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Data You Provide

  • Trip notes, vehicle information, and mileage logs you manually enter
  • Receipt images you scan within the app
  • Cloud account credentials (Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive) for optional sync — only if you connect them

2b. Data Collected Automatically

  • GPS coordinates while the tracking service is active (for trip route mapping)
  • Start/stop timestamps of trips
  • Distance calculations derived from GPS data

2c. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any analytics or crash data
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not sell your data to third parties — ever
  • We do not have access to your cloud files beyond what you explicitly sync

3. Permissions & Why We Use Them

📍 Location (Fine, Coarse & Background)

Required for automatic GPS drive tracking. Fine location captures precise route data. Background location is used so tracking continues while the app is minimized or the screen is off — essential for complete trip logging without manual intervention. You can stop tracking at any time from the notification or app interface.

📷 Camera & Media Access

Used for the receipt scanning feature powered by Google ML Kit OCR. All text recognition is performed entirely on-device — receipt images are never uploaded to any server. You can capture receipts directly or select images from your gallery.

🔔 Notifications

Used to display a persistent notification while GPS tracking is active (required by Android for foreground services). Also used for trip completion summaries and sync status alerts.

📡 Bluetooth

Optional. Used to detect connection/disconnection of Bluetooth devices (e.g., car hands-free systems or OBD adapters) to automatically start or stop trip tracking. Bluetooth data is not stored or transmitted.

⚡ Boot Receiver (Auto-start)

Allows the tracking service to automatically resume after a device reboot, if tracking was active before shutdown. This ensures no trips are missed. You can disable auto-tracking in app settings.

🌐 Internet & Network State

Used only when you choose to sync your trip data or receipts to Dropbox, Google Drive, or Microsoft OneDrive. The app works fully offline without this permission being exercised.

🔋 Battery Optimization Exemption

RiderLog may request exemption from battery optimization to prevent Android from killing the GPS tracking service during long trips. This is purely to ensure reliable mileage tracking and has no effect on data privacy.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • To calculate trip distance and generate mileage reports for tax purposes
  • To extract expense data from receipt images (on-device OCR only)
  • To sync your data to your personally chosen cloud storage, when you opt in
  • To automatically resume tracking after device reboots, if enabled
  • To send you trip-related notifications when the tracking service is running

5. Cloud Storage & Third-Party Services

RiderLog optionally integrates with the following third-party cloud storage services for backup and sync purposes. These integrations are entirely opt-in:

  • Google Drive — governed by Google Privacy Policy
  • Dropbox — governed by Dropbox Privacy Policy
  • Microsoft OneDrive — governed by Microsoft Privacy Statement

We only access the specific folder(s) you authorize during the OAuth connection process. We do not read, store, or transmit any other files in your cloud accounts.

Google ML Kit

Receipt scanning uses Google ML Kit Text Recognition, which runs fully on-device. No images are sent to Google's servers. See ML Kit Privacy disclosure.

Google Play Billing

Subscription purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled entirely by Google. We receive only a confirmation of your subscription status. See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Data Storage & Security

Your trip data, logs, and receipts are stored locally on your device using secure app-private storage. This data is not accessible to other apps on your device.

If you enable cloud sync, your data is transferred over encrypted HTTPS connections to your chosen cloud storage provider. We use OAuth 2.0 for secure authentication with cloud providers — we never store your cloud account passwords.

8. Data Retention & Deletion

All your trip data remains on your device and under your control. You can delete individual trips, receipts, or all app data at any time within the app settings.

Uninstalling RiderLog from your device will remove all locally stored data. If you have synced data to cloud storage, you can delete those files at any time through your cloud provider's interface.

9. Children's Privacy

RiderLog is designed for professional use by adults (18+) and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

10. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data stored on your device
  • Deletion: Delete all your data through the app settings or by uninstalling
  • Portability: Export your trip data in CSV/PDF format from within the app
  • Opt-out: Disable background tracking, cloud sync, or Bluetooth triggers at any time
